## Brand & Style

### Brand Personality & Core Mood
The design system embodies the serene grandeur, tactile authenticity, and ancestral sophistication of a luxury colonial boutique estancia in the Calchaquí Valleys. It communicates timeless hospitality, stillness, and regional pride through the lens of modern editorial restraint. The experience feels welcoming yet elevated: the quiet luxury of whitewashed adobe walls, deep red wine cellars, polished quebracho wood, and golden high-altitude sunsets.

### Target Audience & Emotional Response
The target audience consists of affluent global travelers, oenophiles, and design-conscious explorers seeking authentic cultural immersion without sacrificing world-class comfort. The interface must evoke:
- **Serenity & Slowness:** Inviting the guest to pause and breathe.
- **Architectural Groundedness:** Tangible, enduring, rooted in centuries of Salteño tradition.
- **Sensory Warmth:** Rich textures of sun-baked clay, dark Malbec grapes, woven sheep's wool, and mountain minerals.

### Design Movement & Aesthetic Direction
A tailored synthesis of **Editorial Luxury** and **Warm Tactility**:
- Generous, deliberate white/sand space allowing high-resolution atmospheric imagery to breathe.
- Low-contrast, organic framing evocative of colonial archways and courtyard cloisters.
- Textural accents inspired by Andean looms (*barracán* weave patterns as hairline rules or subtle container dividers).
- Restrained interactions with smooth, deliberate transitions rather than snappy or synthetic animations.

## Colors

### Palette Philosophy
The palette is deeply rooted in the geological and cultural landscape of Cafayate and Cachi:
- **Primary — Vino Tinto / Quebracho (`#7B1E2B`, with `#58111A` for deep states):** Evokes the body of aged Malbec and Tannat, sun-cured leather, and old colonial timber beams. Used for high-emphasis CTAs, prominent brand marks, and key narrative accents.
- **Secondary — Terracota Calchaquí (`#C86D51`):** Represents sun-baked clay tiles, regional ceramics, and evening cliffside reflections. Used for interactive highlights, active badges, and warm focal points.
- **Tertiary — Verde Cardón (`#5C6B50`):** An earthy, desaturated botanical green drawn from desert giant cacti and high-altitude aromatic herbs. Used for subtle status indicators, sustainability badges, and nature-inspired touches.
- **Neutral & Surface Foundations:**
  - **Base Canvas (`#FAF7F2`):** A limewashed, radiant adobe cream that prevents harsh digital starkness.
  - **Surface Container (`#F4EBE1`):** Sun-dried arena earth for structural cards and panels.
  - **Surface Variant / Border (`#ECE2D4`):** Soft stone mortar used for hairline divides.
  - **Carbon Suave (`#2B2625`):** A warm, softened soot-charcoal for typography and micro-borders, replacing harsh black with organic depth.

### Usage Ratios & Accessibility
- **Dominant Canvas (65%):** Sand and adobe surfaces (`#FAF7F2`, `#F4EBE1`) provide open breathing room.
- **Text & Structure (25%):** Carbon Suave (`#2B2625`) guarantees AAA compliance against all sand and adobe layers.
- **Regional Accents (10%):** Rich wine (`#7B1E2B`) and terracotta (`#C86D51`) reserved exclusively for decisive focal elements, navigation anchors, and booking actions.

## Typography

### Structural Pairings & Hierarchy
- **Primary Display & Headlines (`Playfair Display`):** Conveys the intellectual grace of classical Argentine literature, vinicultural tasting books, and colonial architecture. It should be typeset primarily in normal and medium weights with relaxed tracking to preserve its high-contrast stroke anatomy.
- **Body & Functional UI (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Provides a clean, modern, and human touch with geometric clarity. It counters the ornate nature of the serif headlines, making room descriptions, wine lists, reservation flows, and pricing effortlessly legible.

### Editorial Styling Principles
- Overlines and category tags use `label-sm` set in all-caps with generous character spacing (`0.1em`), paired with wine or cardón tones.
- Long-form narratives (e.g., vineyard histories, estancia suites) prioritize `body-lg` at 18px with relaxed leading (28px) against `#FAF7F2` backgrounds for optimal reading comfort.

## Layout & Spacing

### Layout Philosophy
The layout adheres to an **asymmetric editorial grid** combined with a robust 12-column foundation for responsive utility.
- **Desktop (>= 1280px):** 12-column layout, max-width constrained to `1360px` with generous `4rem` outer margins. Modules utilize asymmetrical balance (e.g., 7 columns of photography paired with 5 columns of narrative room descriptions).
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8-column layout, `2rem` outer margins, collapsing complex multi-column pairings into rhythmic vertical cards.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `1.25rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Interactive actions remain grounded within thumb reach via fixed bottom utility panels.

### Rhythm & Vertical Spacing
- Structural page sections are separated by `space-2xl` (mobile) to `space-3xl` (desktop), deliberately mimicking the expansive vistas of the valley.
- Component-level spacing relies on strict intervals of 8px, utilizing `space-md` for interior card padding and `space-xl` between related editorial modules.

## Elevation & Depth

### Atmospheric Tonal Depth
In keeping with natural adobe and stone architecture, traditional stark drop shadows are replaced by **tonal layering and warm diffused ambient occlusion**:
- **Layer 0 (Base Canvas):** Limewashed adobe `#FAF7F2`.
- **Layer 1 (Cards, Modules, Groupings):** Sand surface `#F4EBE1` paired with a delicate hairline border (`1px solid #ECE2D4`).
- **Layer 2 (Overlays, Floating Navigation, Booking Drawers):** High-opacity tinted surface (`rgba(250, 247, 242, 0.94)` with an 8px backdrop blur) accompanied by an organic sun-warmed shadow:
  `0 16px 32px -8px rgba(43, 38, 37, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(123, 30, 43, 0.04)`.

### Border & Divider Accents
- Dividers use hairline strokes (`1px`) in `#ECE2D4`.
- For signature hero dividers and footers, introduce an optional Andean textile-inspired dashed line motif (`stroke-dasharray: 4, 8`) rendered in `#C86D51` at 50% opacity.

## Shapes

### Shape Language: Softened Colonial Masonry
The design avoids hyper-sharp industrial edges and overly synthetic pills, opting instead for **Level 1 (Soft)**:
- Standard elements (buttons, inputs, cards) use `0.25rem` (4px) to `0.5rem` (8px) corner radii, mimicking hand-carved quebracho wood and smoothed stucco plaster.
- Large containers, image viewports, and modal sheets scale to `rounded-lg` (0.5rem) or `rounded-xl` (0.75rem).
- Curated architectural hero images may feature an authentic colonial arched top (`border-radius: 9999px 9999px 4px 4px`) to mirror the traditional arcade corridors (*galerías*) of Salteño estancias.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Reservations & Key CTAs):** Solid `#7B1E2B` background with `#FAF7F2` text, `4px` corner radius. Hover state smoothly transitions to `#58111A`. Padding: `0.875rem 1.75rem`. Typography: `label-lg`.
- **Secondary Action (Discoveries & Wine Cellar):** Transparent background with a `1.5px` border in `#7B1E2B`, text in `#7B1E2B`. On hover, fills with `#F4EBE1`.
- **Tertiary / Editorial Link:** Text-only in `#C86D51`, accompanied by an elegant trailing arrow glyph (`→`) that shifts 4px on hover, underlined with a subtle offset rule.

### Cards (Suites, Experiences, Gastronomy)
- Built on `#F4EBE1` with a `1px` border in `#ECE2D4` and `roundedness: 1`.
- Image aspect ratios are standardized to classical formats: 4:3 for suite interiors, 16:9 for landscape panoramas, and 3:2 for culinary stories.
- Imagery features an ambient transition: an imperceptible warm sepia tint on rest that clears to vibrant natural richness on cursor hover.

### Chips & Badges
- **Heritage & Terroir Badges:** Outlined pill chips with `0.75rem` padding, background `#FAF7F2`, border `1px solid #5C6B50`, text `#5C6B50` (`label-sm`, uppercase).
- **Amenity Chips:** Soft `#ECE2D4` fill with `#2B2625` text, used to communicate suite features (e.g., "Hogar a leña", "Cava privada", "Vista al Nevado").

### Form Inputs & Date Selectors
- **Input Fields:** Generous touch target (48px height), background `#FAF7F2`, border `1px solid #ECE2D4`, text `#2B2625`. 
- **Focus State:** Border shifts cleanly to `#7B1E2B` with an ambient glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(123, 30, 43, 0.15)`).
- **Date Pickers & Guest Counters:** High-contrast serif numerals for selected calendar dates, highlighting check-in and check-out ranges with a soft Terracotta band (`rgba(200, 109, 81, 0.18)`).

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Radio markers use concentric circles: an outer ring in `#7B1E2B` and an inner filled circle in `#7B1E2B` separated by a 2px `#FAF7F2` spacer.
- Checkboxes feature a custom checkmark path with a slight calligraphic taper rather than a geometric stroke.

### Specialized Hospitality Components
- **The Reservation Bar (Sticky Availability Anchor):** A dockable bottom or header bar featuring segmented date, guest, and suite selectors on a frosted adobe background (`rgba(244, 235, 225, 0.96)`), punctuated by the primary wine-toned booking trigger.
- **Tasting Notes / Terroir Card:** A dual-sided card interface designed for the estancia’s Torrontés and Malbec collections, displaying altitude meters (m.s.n.m.), barrel aging months, and pairing recommendations in clean tabular typography.
